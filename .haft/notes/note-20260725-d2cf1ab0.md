# Fiction/narrative writing is explicitly out of scope for /ps-intake

## Observations

- Tested /ps-intake with an idea framed as a detective story ('the-clue-that-shouldn-t-count'). Bundle's content contract (thesis/killer_sections-vs-competing-coverage/counterintuitive-findings/key_questions) is entirely nonfiction-shaped, inherited from the Medium POC's blog-post pipeline -- there is no fiction branch.
- Given fiction input, intake did not fail or ask -- it silently substituted a nonfiction craft essay ABOUT detective-fiction technique (thesis, killer sections, counterintuitive findings, an essay-structured outline) instead of scaffolding an actual story (no premise, characters, plot, or scene structure). The genre substitution was never surfaced to the operator.
- Named-authority grounding (via ps-factcheck) still worked correctly even in this wrong-genre output -- real citations for Knox's Decalogue and Van Dine's Rules -- confirming the grounding mechanism is robust independent of genre; the gap is entirely at the content-model level.

## Rationale

Operator decision: fiction needs a genuinely different content model (premise, character want/need, conflict, scene-level structure) -- building that now would be speculative investment for a use case that's surfaced as one stress-test probe, not a stated goal, consistent with this session's other deferrals (SHARPEN, full POC-style research pipeline). Excluding fiction now, rather than either half-supporting it in the nonfiction schema or building a separate engine, mirrors the project's existing precedent of explicit target-system-scope exclusion (multi-author support: prob-20260718-97b5a2a3). Fix: intake must detect fiction/narrative requests and decline explicitly, never silently reframe them as a nonfiction essay about the same topic.
