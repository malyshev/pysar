# 

## Rationale

Two findings, both load-bearing for how to read this comparison (and future ones like it):

1. Pipeline-stage mismatch, not a quality signal. POC's article.md is the output of intake+research+draft+staff-edit+sharpen+optimize+humanize (confirmed by staff-edit-changelog.md, sharpen-changelog.md, humanize-changelog.md, seo-changelog.md, site-publish-changelog.md all present in that directory). Pysar's article.md is intake+draft only -- run-log.jsonl shows exactly two passes (intake, draft), no research pass ran. Comparing a 6-pass-polished final artifact against a 1-pass first draft and asking "which is better" conflates pipeline maturity with system quality. Pysar has no staff-edit/sharpen/humanize/optimize passes built yet, so this gap is expected, not a Pysar defect.

2. Real, concrete gap: intake.Source (internal/intake/bundle.go) has only {URL, Note} -- no Shortname field, unlike research.Source. This piece's one grounded fact (node:24-slim's LTS currency, verified against https://endoflife.date/nodejs during intake's Step 4) has no citable shortname under /ps-draft's [^shortname]-only citation convention, and the draft correctly emitted zero citations rather than inventing one or citing a raw URL (draft.Validate would have rejected a raw URL or an unmatched shortname). So a real, honestly-fetched source exists but never becomes a visible citation in the final draft -- an intake-grounded fact currently has no path to attribution unless a /ps-research pass also runs. Worth a future fix: either give intake.Source a Shortname too, or have intake's grounded sources merge into the same shortname space /ps-research writes to.

Declined to produce a "Pysar is N% better" figure -- that implies a validated metric that doesn't exist here; it would be false precision over a mostly qualitative (voice/polish) judgment. On raw technical reasoning within the single-draft-pass layer, Pysar's draft was comparably or more precise on two or three specific points (cache-order mechanics with real numbers, multi-stage-build necessity tied to native deps, current LTS choice vs POC's more dated node:20-alpine) but has less voice-polish and zero resolved citations, both because the pipeline stages that would produce that polish don't exist yet, not because of a worse draft.
