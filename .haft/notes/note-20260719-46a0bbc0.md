## Rationale

Operator ran /ps-voice live and got the old pre-template conversation flow, initially framed as "run full FPF cycle to decide" -- diagnosis (grep + hash comparison of the real ~/.claude/skills/ps-voice/SKILL.md against repo source) found no design ambiguity at all: the shipped Step 1a was correct, just never deployed to the machine that would actually run it. A full explore/compare cycle here would have been ceremony with nothing to decide (matches feedback_decision_ceremony_calibration.md's existing guidance). The generalizable lesson: real-binary verification in an isolated scratchpad fake-home proves the mechanism works, but does not prove the operator's own environment reflects it -- those are two different claims, and only the first was actually being checked. Going forward, after a decision whose blast radius includes ~/.claude/skills or ~/.pysar, also reinstall + refresh the operator's real global state before declaring the work done, not just a sandboxed copy.
