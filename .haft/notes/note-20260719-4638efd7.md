# 

## Rationale

This came from a straightforward logical inconsistency the operator caught directly (not a design ambiguity worth an FPF cycle): Step 6's own precondition never considered which of Step 1b's two exit paths (as-is vs. edited) or Step 2's blank-conversation path produced the profile being offered. The general lesson: any step that fires "after a successful save" needs to account for every path that can reach it, not just the one it was originally written for -- Step 6 was written before Step 1b existed and never revisited when Step 1b was added.
