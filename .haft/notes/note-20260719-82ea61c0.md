# 

## Rationale

Operator's own question exposed the gap directly: "what profit of this information? what it deliver to user?" -- a fair challenge that plain-language wording alone doesn't survive. A fact with no available action is noise regardless of how it's phrased; making jargon readable doesn't make a non-actionable fact worth reporting. This generalizes the "stay silent when nothing to say" principle (already applied to the matches case) to the differs case too, once it's clear neither case gives the author something to do.
