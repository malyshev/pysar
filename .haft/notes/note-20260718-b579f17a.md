# V4 CLI registry wall-clock was ~36s, not 45–90 minutes

## Observations

- Agent estimated V4 (thin internal/cli registry) at ~45–90 minutes and V1 (static banner) at ~15–30 minutes when advising on sol-20260718-06d87720 / dec-20260718-c4cd8f62.
- Measured implementation of V4 under dec-20260718-c4cd8f62 took 36.088 seconds wall-clock (test + install + smoke included).
- The multi-tens-of-minutes delta was invented cost inflation, not an observed engineering fact — it overstated V4's spend relative to V1 and should not be reused in future CLI or 'near-zero cost structure' comparisons.

## Rationale

Operator correctly called out that the earlier '40–45 min vs 15 min' framing was a lie relative to reality. Cost claims used to score variants must be calibrated to measured or honestly bounded effort; fabricating hour-scale costs for ~80 LOC of stdlib Go recreates the same anti-pattern as 'premature abstraction' — false prudence that biases toward hardcoding.
