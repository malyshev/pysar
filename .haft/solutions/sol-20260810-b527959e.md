# Solutions for: Final exported prose still contains unresolved [^shortname] citation markers

Problem: prob-20260810-70678bfc

## Variants (5)

### V1. Mechanical resolve-at-export from sources.md

export_piece_to_root (or a shared helper it calls) mechanically replaces every [^shortname] with [anchor](url) from sources.md before writing the root file. No LLM SEO packaging; fails closed if a marker has no source. Piece stage files (sharpen/humanize) may still hold markers.

**Strengths:**
- Hard fail-closed on export
- Works for --research without --seo
- Reuses sources.md as single URL truth

**Novelty:** Fixes the author-facing surface at the last gate without forcing --seo or changing humanize’s preserve-markers rule for non-SEO pieces.

**Weakest link:** Export becomes a silent second SEO-lite; authors who wanted markers for a later manual SEO pass lose them only in the export copy — but if they re-export after editing humanize, behavior must stay consistent and URLs must never be invented.

**Risks:**
- Anchor-text choice is heuristic without SEO pass
- Diverges export body from humanize.md

### V2. Always run citation-resolve (SEO link step) before humanize when markers exist

If the revision entering humanize still has [^…] markers, /ps (and standalone humanize precondition) requires a resolve pass — either full /ps-seo or a thinner resolve-only stage — so humanize never sees unresolved carriers on the path to export.

**Strengths:**
- Matches existing seo.md zero-marker validator
- humanize.md and export stay identical

**Novelty:** Moves cleanliness into the pipeline order rather than a last-mile export rewrite; keeps humanize’s ‘preserve when no SEO’ rule honest by ensuring SEO/resolve ran when needed.

**Weakest link:** Couples research-heavy pieces to an SEO-shaped stage even when the author did not opt into --seo packaging (title/meta), unless resolve-only is split from discoverability packaging.

**Risks:**
- Scope creep into always-on SEO packaging
- Needs clear split of resolve vs CTR packaging

### V3. Export fails closed if any [^shortname] remains

export_piece_to_root rejects when the chosen revision still contains [^…] markers, forcing the author/agent to run /ps-seo (or resolve) first. No automatic rewrite.

**Strengths:**
- Tiny blast radius
- No invented anchors
- Reuses existing SEO tooling

**Novelty:** Minimal product change: surface the contract violation instead of inventing resolution policy at export.

**Weakest link:** Dogfood with --research and no --seo becomes a hard stop at export — surprising unless /ps auto-invokes resolve or docs make --seo mandatory whenever research cites.

**Risks:**
- Friction for research-only pieces
- Agents may strip markers instead of resolving

### V4. Strip markers at export (remove carriers, leave prose)

export deletes [^shortname] tokens from the exported body without inserting links. Attribution becomes prose-only unless SEO ran earlier.

**Strengths:**
- Trivial implementation
- Zero garbage tokens

**Novelty:** Guarantees no garbage tokens with zero link-resolution policy — opposite of resolve.

**Weakest link:** Destroys citation affordances the research pass paid for; final text looks clean but ungrounded — worse honesty failure than visible markers for research-backed claims.

**Risks:**
- Silent loss of sourcing
- Conflicts with research value prop

### V5. /ps --research implies citation resolve before export (orchestrator)

When research_mode is full or markers exist, /ps orchestrator always inserts resolve/SEO before humanize even without --seo flag; --seo still means full discoverability packaging, while research alone triggers resolve-only.

**Strengths:**
- Matches operator expectation for final text
- Keeps --seo as packaging opt-in

**Novelty:** Flag semantics: research creates carriers that the same autopilot must clean; separates ‘must resolve’ from ‘opt-in SEO packaging’.

**Weakest link:** Changes default /ps --research behavior and must not quietly turn on title/meta SEO packaging; needs a distinct resolve stage or a documented resolve-only mode of ps-seo.

**Risks:**
- Orchestrator complexity
- Standalone /ps-humanize on research pieces still needs a gate
