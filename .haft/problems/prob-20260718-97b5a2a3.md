# Exclude multi-author/author-registry model from Pysar's target-system scope

## Signal

a prior writing POC implements a multi-author model (an authors/ registry, per-article author assignment). No current Pysar spec section or bound decision claims multi-author support -- specs consistently say "the author" (singular) -- but the risk of it being reintroduced by a future prior-POC-inspired exploration (CL1 citation) is real: the now-dominated variants of the on-disk-structure comparison (sol-20260718-47044f62) explicitly floated it -- V2's strength was "shared author voice across pieces," V1's risk was "voice/author registry deferred" -- both assumed a future multi-author path. Operator's reasoning: since pysar init scaffolds a distinct project per invocation, each with its own voice, a cross-project author/voice registry is redundant scope, not a missing feature.

## Problem Type

synthesis

## Constraints

- external POCs/haft citations CL1 max, never sole justification (dec-20260718-eafefad2)
- Must not contradict the already-bound on-disk-structure decision (dec-20260718-4b222794), which already has no project-level author concept
- Voice-lock (the editorial pass that removes AI-sounding prose) is unaffected -- this constraint is about identity/ownership model, not the voice-lock pass

## Optimization Targets

- clarity of the single-voice-per-project boundary for future decisions
- minimal governance overhead

## Observation Indicators

- number of new artifacts created to encode this

## Acceptance

A durable, citable artifact exists such that future explorations (memory engine delivery, run-log, any project-root topology revisit) can check against it instead of re-deriving or accidentally reintroducing a multi-author/author-registry model from prior writing POC.

## Blast Radius
