# Memory engine: cross-piece continuity so new pieces don't unknowingly repeat prior openings, examples, or wording

## Signal

An author writing serial/episodic prose in one Pysar project (e.g. a chapter-a-day story, or a run of standalone articles on overlapping topics) can unknowingly repeat specific things across pieces -- a near-identical opening line/scene (e.g. 'I woke up at 2 A.M.' as an opener twice), a reused example or anecdote, or a recurring turn of phrase -- because Pysar currently has no way to check a new piece against the corpus of previously-written pieces in the same project. Nothing in the already-onboarded TS/ES specs covers this: TS.environment-change.001 covers idea-to-ship-ready shaping per piece, not continuity across pieces.

## Problem Type

synthesis

## Constraints

- Must fit inside the already-decided 5-layer functional-core/imperative-shell architecture (ES.enabling.architecture.001) and the Go tech stack (dec-20260718-239680d4) -- no heavy new runtime dependency that contradicts the solo-maintainer, no-CI-yet constraint that decision was made under
- Per the reference-agnosticism gate (dec-20260718-eafefad2): if haft's own search/graph mechanisms are mentioned as inspiration, that stays CL1-capped, never sole justification, with Pysar-original divergence stated
- Must not require the author to manually tag, summarize, or catalog past pieces -- extraction/indexing happens automatically as pieces are written, consistent with the author-surface promise already recorded in TS.environment-change.001

## Acceptance

Given a project with 2+ previously-written pieces, when the author starts or finishes drafting a new piece, Pysar can surface at least: (1) any near-duplicate opening (same hook/scene type or highly similar first lines) against prior pieces, and (2) any reused example, anecdote, or scene across pieces -- each flagged with a reference to which specific prior piece it echoes, as an observable check the author can act on before shipping. This must work without the author manually tagging or cataloging past pieces.

## Blast Radius

Spans the Artifact/state layer (needs to store/index past pieces) and is consulted by the Editorial engine core (functional core) during scaffolding/drafting passes -- touches ES.enabling.architecture.001's layering directly; likely implies a future target-system use case addition (out of scope for this ADR, which is architecture-only)
