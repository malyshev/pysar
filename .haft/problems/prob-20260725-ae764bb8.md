# Revisit: Write intake artifacts now, to a clearly-marked provisional location (not deferred pending prob-20260718-f6ac8127)

## Signal

Decision dec-20260725-35fa2d24 needs re-evaluation: Invariant "no web search / synthesized research at intake time" no longer matches shipped behavior. Operator pushed back on the original reasoning: the constraint conflated two different things — POC's actual rule is "research must not override the author's stated thesis/POV," not "the agent may never verify its own technical claims." Re-examined POC's research-sourcing.mdc and AGENTS.md directly: research-sourcing.mdc explicitly forbids research from touching Thesis/killer-sections/counterintuitive findings (those are intake/practitioner-owned), but says nothing against verifying supporting facts. Implemented: ps-intake now has WebSearch/WebFetch access and a new Step 4 (SKILL.md) instructing the agent to ground genuinely-uncertain technical claims via a small number of targeted fetches before writing killer_sections/counterintuitive/examples -- stable/well-established knowledge still needs no research (the common case). Sources are optional (Bundle.Sources, empty by default) and real research_mode now reflects reality (none/targeted) instead of being hardcoded to none. The operator's thesis/angle stays the framing authority in all cases -- unchanged from the original decision's intent, just no longer conflated with a blanket web-search ban. Needs a formal /h-decide to supersede dec-20260725-35fa2d24 with the corrected invariant.
